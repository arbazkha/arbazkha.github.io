Numerical analysis · partial differential equations

Finite element and spectral methods for problems in elasticity, poroelasticity, and nonlinear flow.

I work on the numerical analysis of partial differential equations, with an emphasis on designing and analysing finite element, spectral element, and discontinuous Galerkin methods — alongside consistent physics-informed neural network (PINN) approaches — and on the optimal control of nonlinear flow problems. Based at the Department of Mathematics, IIT Roorkee.

Research

Research interests

  • Finite and spectral element methods

    Construction and error analysis of conforming, nonconforming, and spectral element methods for elliptic and parabolic PDEs, including problems with corner singularities and unbounded domains.

  • Mixed methods and eigenvalue problems

    Mixed and pseudostress-based finite element formulations for elasticity, Stokes, and poroelasticity eigenvalue problems, including locking-free and heterogeneous-media formulations.

  • Discontinuous Galerkin & nonconforming methods

    Interior penalty and DG discretisations for nearly incompressible elasticity and for reaction–diffusion equations with weakly singular kernels, such as the delayed Burgers–Huxley equation.

  • Optimal control of nonlinear flows

    Well-posedness, regularity, and control of doubly diffusive and other coupled nonlinear flow models on bounded Lipschitz domains.

  • Preconditioning & uncertainty quantification

    Robust preconditioners for stochastic Galerkin formulations of parameter-dependent elasticity and related PDE systems.

  • Consistent PINNs for PDEs

    Physics-informed neural network methods with provable consistency and error bounds for elliptic and parabolic PDEs, obstacle problems, and pressure-robust incompressible flow formulations.
